## Definition
A profile mismatch is account activity far above, or very different from, what the customer declared at onboarding: occupation, monthly income and expected activity. KYC is the baseline every alert is judged against. When money moving through the account cannot be reconciled with who the customer says they are, the source of funds is unknown, and unknown source of funds is the core money laundering risk.

## Red flags
- Monthly inflow above 2.5 times declared monthly income; above 5 times is a strong indicator.
- One or more large single credits with no matching event in KYC.
- A new type of activity: a salaried customer receiving many business-like transfers, or a student making large international payments.
- Inflows from counterparties unrelated to the declared employer or family.
- Repeated mismatches over several months rather than a single spike.

## Common benign explanations
- An annual bonus, commission or end-of-service gratuity from the known employer.
- Sale of a property, car or other asset, with the sale agreement or transfer recorded in KYC.
- An inheritance, a gift from family, or the maturity of an investment, documented.
- A change of job or salary increase that has not yet been reflected in the declared income.
- Loan disbursement from a recognised lender.

## What to check
1. Declared monthly income, occupation and expected activity.
2. Total inflow in the alert window versus declared income, and versus the 90-day baseline.
3. The largest credits: who sent them, what the reference says, and whether the sender is known (employer, family, buyer of an asset).
4. KYC notes for a recorded event and its date, and whether the date and amount match the credit.
5. Whether the excess funds stayed in the account or were moved out quickly (which raises pass-through risk).

## Evidence needed to close
- A specific event recorded in KYC notes (sale, bonus, inheritance) quoted exactly, with an amount and date that match the credit within a reasonable margin.
- The credit comes from a counterparty consistent with that event.
- No unexplained further inflows in the window.
Cite the credit transaction ID and the KYC wording. A note saying documents were requested but not yet received is not evidence; route to Needs more info.

## Evidence needed to escalate
Escalate when inflow is far above declared income with no recorded explanation, when the mismatch repeats across months, or when the funds come from unrelated parties and are quickly moved on. Cite the largest credits and any onward transfers.
